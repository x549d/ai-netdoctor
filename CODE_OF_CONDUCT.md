# AI NetDoctor Code of Conduct

[简体中文](#简体中文)

## Scope and standards

Everyone should be able to participate in AI NetDoctor without harassment or discrimination. This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and project communications. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project in an official capacity.

- Treat people of all backgrounds and levels of experience with respect. Welcome different perspectives, give specific and constructive feedback, and take responsibility for the impact of your actions.
- Harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory language or conduct, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, and retaliation for reporting are prohibited. Sharing another person's private information without consent is also prohibited.

## Private reports and review

Send conduct reports to **[MAINTAINER TO ADD A PRIVATE CONDUCT REPORTING CHANNEL]**.

Describe what happened, when and where it occurred, and any relevant links or redacted evidence. Keep incident details out of public issues. If a report concerns the person who would normally receive it, use **[MAINTAINER TO ADD AN ALTERNATE PRIVATE REPORTING CHANNEL]**.

Maintainers are responsible for communicating these standards and reviewing reports promptly and fairly. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must step aside. Keep the reporter's identity and report details private, share information only as needed to handle the incident, and communicate the outcome where appropriate.

## Moderation and consequences

Maintainers may hide or remove harmful content and take proportionate action. Depending on severity, impact, and prior behavior, actions may include a private correction, a warning with clear conditions, a temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions. Explain the decision to the affected parties when appropriate, while protecting the reporter's privacy.

## 简体中文

### 适用范围与行为规范

每个人都应能在免受骚扰和歧视的环境中参与 AI NetDoctor。本准则适用于 Issue、PR、代码审查和项目沟通等社区空间，也适用于以官方身份公开代表项目的人员。

- 尊重不同背景和经验水平的参与者。欢迎不同观点，提出具体且有建设性的反馈，并为自身行为产生的影响承担责任。
- 禁止骚扰、威胁、恐吓、歧视性言行、不受欢迎的性暗示或追求、人身攻击及对举报者的报复。禁止未经同意公开他人的私人信息。

### 私密举报与受理

私密举报渠道为 **[待维护者填写私密行为准则举报渠道]**。

请说明发生了什么、时间、地点，并附相关链接或脱敏证据。请勿在公开 Issue 中披露事件详情。如果举报涉及通常负责受理的人，请使用 **[待维护者填写备用私密举报渠道]**。

维护者负责向社区说明行为规范，及时、公平地审查举报。存在利益冲突的维护者必须回避。保护举报人身份和报告详情，仅在处理事件所需的范围内共享信息，并在适当情况下告知处理结果。

### 内容管理与处理措施

维护者可以隐藏或移除有害内容，并采取相称措施。视严重程度、影响和过往行为，可私下纠正、发出附带明确要求的警告、暂时限制参与或永久禁止参与。严重事件可以立即采取限制措施。在保护举报人隐私的前提下，适当向相关当事人解释决定。
